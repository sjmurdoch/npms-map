# Archive

Finished work that the app does not use. Nothing here is loaded by `index.html`, `app.js` or `sw.js`, and nothing here is fetched or cached at runtime.

- **`species-extraction.tar.gz`** — the NPMS *Species Identification Guide* extracted to structured data: 410 species with their broad habitats, difficulty class, Wildflower-level flag, flowering months and field notes, plus the script that produced it. Built for a species-recording feature that was cut from scope. Unpack it for the full write-up, including how the habitat pictograms were decoded and the one distinction the guide itself does not make.
